FROM SIGHT TO SOUND

Earth science, with a different sense.

Each beat samples a vertical strip of the image. Average color selects a pentatonic note; brightness controls loudness. Explore the resulting melody at your own pace, or upload an EIC visualization frame to hear its patterns.
